💽 Hard drive (internal HDD) · 🔍 Not detected / not showing up

Hard Drive Not Showing Up or Not Detected

A drive that does not show up may have a loose cable, a bad port, a lost partition, or a real hardware fault. The first checks are simple and safe. If the drive is silent, clicking, or hot, the cause may be physical.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Varies

Cable and software checks are safe, but repeated power-ups of a physically failing drive can make the damage worse.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not keep powering the drive on and off to see if it comes back. Each attempt can add wear if it is failing.
  • Do not click Initialize or Format if your computer offers it. That can erase the data.
  • Do not open the drive. Hard drives need a cleanroom, and dust ruins them.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the drive is missing from the BIOS after trying a new cable and port, or it makes any unusual noise, stop and consider a professional with a cleanroom.

Questions people ask

My drive shows up but says Raw or needs formatting. What now?

Do not format it. The file table may be damaged, and TestDisk or PhotoRec can often still reach your files, ideally working on an image.

Could it just be a cable?

Yes, that is very common. Try another cable, port, and power connector before assuming the drive is dead.

Can I fix it by swapping the circuit board?

Not as a DIY fix. Each board has a ROM chip with data unique to that drive, which must be moved onto the new board, so leave it to specialists.
