💽 Hard drive (internal HDD) · 🪫 Won't power on / dead

Hard Drive Not Spinning or Won't Power On

If the drive makes no sound and does not spin, the cause may be a bad cable, a weak power supply, or a failed circuit board or motor. A burnt smell points to an electrical fault, which can be dangerous to keep trying.

4/5 · High risk
Success odds: Low

Cable and power checks are fine, but repeated attempts on a drive with a burnt board or stuck motor can cause more harm.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Unplug it right away if you smell burning or see scorch marks. Do not power it up again.
  • Do not switch it on and off again and again hoping it starts. Limit yourself to one or two careful tries.
  • Do not use the freezer trick, and never open the drive. It needs a cleanroom.
  • Do not swap circuit boards yourself. Each board has a ROM chip with data unique to that drive, which must be moved over, and that is a job for a lab.

Your steps

0 of 7 done
  1. Show me how
    1. Shut down and unplug the drive. Turn it over so you can see the green circuit board on the underside.
    2. Look for dark scorch spots, a cracked or blackened chip, and sniff for a burnt smell.
    3. If you see or smell any, leave it unplugged and skip to the last step.
  2. Show me how
    1. Plug the drive's power into a different connector from the same power supply, or try it in another computer or a USB dock.
    2. Check that other drives on the same cable work. If none do, the power supply is the problem.
  3. Show me how
    1. Use a spare SATA data cable and a different port on the motherboard, or a USB dock.
    2. Press each connector in firmly before switching on.
  4. Show me how
    1. Switch on and rest a fingertip lightly on the drive for about 10 seconds.
    2. Hum or vibration: it spins, so check whether it appears in the BIOS or Disk Management.
    3. Nothing at all: the fault is power, circuit board or motor. Switch off, do not retry.
  5. Show me how
    1. Plug in a healthy drive at least as big as this one.
    2. Follow our imaging guide with ddrescue (on Linux), or HDDSuperClone. Always type the failing drive first and the empty destination second. If you swap them, the copy erases your data.

    1. Run TestDisk to see folders and names, then PhotoRec if needed.
    2. Always point the tools at the image file, never at the original drive.

Questions people ask

My drive is dead but not clicking. Is that better or worse?

Neither is good. Silence points to power, circuit board or motor trouble. Check the cable and power first, then consider a professional.

Can I replace the circuit board myself?

Not as a DIY fix. The board's ROM chip holds data unique to that drive and must be moved onto the new board, which experts do with special tools.

Is a burnt smell always the end?

Not always. Sometimes only a small protection part on the board has blown and the data is fine, but it needs a careful repair by an expert.
