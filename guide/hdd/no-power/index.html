💽 Hard drive (internal HDD) · 🪫 Won't power on / dead

Hard Drive Not Spinning or Won't Power On

If the drive makes no sound and does not spin, the cause may be a bad cable, bad power supply, or a failed circuit board or motor. A burnt smell suggests an electrical fault, which can be dangerous to keep trying.

4/5 · High risk
Success odds: Low

Simple cable and power checks are fine, but repeated attempts on a drive with a burnt board or stuck motor can cause more harm.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Unplug it right away if you smell burning or see scorch marks. Do not power it up again.
  • Do not power-cycle it again and again hoping it starts. Limit yourself to one or two careful tries.
  • Do not use the freezer trick, and never open the drive. It needs a cleanroom.
  • Do not swap circuit boards yourself. Each board has a ROM chip with drive-specific data that must be moved over, which is a job for a lab.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If it does not spin after trying another cable and power source, or there is a burnt smell or scorch marks, stop and use a professional.

Questions people ask

My drive is dead but not clicking. Is that better or worse?

Neither is good. Silence points to power, circuit board or motor trouble. Check the cable and power first, then consider a professional.

Can I replace the circuit board myself?

Not as a DIY fix. The board's ROM chip holds unique data for that drive and must be moved to the new board, which experts do with special tools.

Is a burnt smell always the end?

Not always. Sometimes only the protection part on the board has blown and the data is fine, but it needs a careful repair by an expert.
