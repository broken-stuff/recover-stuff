💽 Hard drive (internal HDD) · 🔊 Clicking or grinding noises

Hard Drive Clicking, Grinding or Beeping

Clicking, grinding or beeping usually means the moving parts inside, such as the read/write heads (the tiny arms that read the disk), are damaged or stuck. Every extra power-up can scratch the disk surface and destroy more data.

5/5 · Pro territory
Success odds: Low

Mechanical faults can get worse every time the drive is powered on, and real repairs need a cleanroom.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Power it off and leave it off. Do not keep trying, because each power-up can scratch the disk inside.
  • Do not put the drive in the freezer, tap it or shake it. The freezer trick is a myth and can cause water damage from condensation.
  • Never open the drive. Dust in a normal room ruins the disk.
  • Do not run recovery scans, CHKDSK or any repair tool on it. Heavy reading stresses a failing drive, and making a copy (see below) is the only DIY step worth trying.

Questions people ask

Does the freezer trick work?

No. It can cause condensation, which is water damage, and make things worse. Data recovery experts advise against it.

Can I open the drive and fix it myself?

No. Opening a hard drive outside a cleanroom lets in dust that scratches the disk. Replacing the heads needs special tools and matching parts.

Is it worth trying once myself?

Only if the data is not critical. Keep it to one careful attempt, and make an image straight away if it works.
