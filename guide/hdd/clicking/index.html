💽 Hard drive (internal HDD) · 🔊 Clicking or grinding noises

Hard Drive Clicking, Grinding or Beeping

Clicking, grinding or beeping usually means the moving parts inside, such as the read/write heads, are damaged or stuck. Every extra power-up can scratch the disk surface and destroy more data.

5/5 · Pro territory
Success odds: Low

Mechanical faults can get worse every time the drive is powered on, and real repairs need a cleanroom.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Power it off and leave it off. Do not keep trying, because each power-up can scratch the platters.
  • Do not put the drive in the freezer, tap it, or shake it. The freezer trick is a myth that can cause condensation damage.
  • Never open the drive. Dust in a normal room ruins the platters.
  • Do not run recovery scans, CHKDSK or other repair tools on it. Heavy reading stresses a failing drive; imaging (below) is the only DIY step worth trying.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the data matters and the drive clicks, grinds, beeps or will not spin, stop and have a professional with a cleanroom look at it.

Questions people ask

Does the freezer trick work?

No. It can cause condensation and make the damage worse. Experts and data recovery communities advise against it.

Can I open the drive and fix it myself?

No. Opening a hard drive outside a cleanroom lets in dust that can scratch the platters. Head replacement needs special tools and matching parts.

Is it worth trying once myself?

Only if the data is not critical. Keep it to one careful attempt, and image the drive immediately if it works.
