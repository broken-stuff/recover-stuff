💽 Hard drive (internal HDD) · 🧩 Corrupted / RAW / asks to format

Fix a Corrupted or Unreadable Hard Drive

Corruption means the drive's file records are damaged, often after a power loss, a bad shutdown, or failing sectors. The files may still be there. Do not run repair tools on the original first, because they can change data.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Medium

The drive may be weakening, and repair tools like CHKDSK on the original can change data before you have a copy.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not run CHKDSK, fsck, or Disk Utility repair on the original before copying your data. Repairs can rewrite data.
  • Do not click Format when Windows says the drive needs to be formatted.
  • Do not install recovery software onto the affected drive, and do not save recovered files back to it.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If imaging keeps stalling, the drive makes noises, or the data is critical, stop and talk to a professional rather than hammering the drive.

Questions people ask

Should I run CHKDSK to fix it?

Not first. It can rewrite damaged data. Make an image of the drive, then repair or recover from that copy.

What is a disk image?

It is an exact copy of everything on the drive saved as a file or onto another drive. It lets you try recovery over and over without risking the original.

Why does it say the drive is RAW?

The computer cannot read the file system, usually due to damage. The data is often still there and TestDisk may restore it.
