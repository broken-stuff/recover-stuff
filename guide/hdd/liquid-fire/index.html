💽 Hard drive (internal HDD) · 🔥 Water, fire or physical damage

Water, Liquid or Fire Damaged Hard Drive

Liquid can short the circuit board and corrode parts inside, and fire or heat can warp the drive. Powering it on can cause a short or scratch the platters, so the safest move is to keep it off and get professional help.

5/5 · Pro territory
Success odds: Low

Power, heat, or opening the drive can turn recoverable damage into permanent loss.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not power it on. Not even once to see if it works.
  • Do not dry it with a hair dryer, oven or heater, and do not bury it in rice. Heat can warp parts, and rice adds dust and starch without drying the inside.
  • Do not open the drive or try to clean the platters.
  • Do not shake it or rinse it to remove dirt or salt water.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

Water, liquid, smoke or fire damage should go to a professional with a cleanroom before anyone powers the drive on.

Questions people ask

Will putting it in rice help?

No. Rice does not pull water out of a drive, and it can add dust and starch. Keep it off and get professional help.

Can I dry it with a hair dryer?

No. Heat can warp parts and drive moisture further inside. Leave drying and cleaning to an expert.

What if it was fully dry and looks fine?

Looking fine is not proof it is safe, because residue can short the board. Powering it is a gamble, so a professional is safer for important data.
