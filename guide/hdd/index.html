💽 Hard drive (internal HDD)

Hard drive (internal HDD) data recovery: what happened?

Spinning magnetic disk inside a desktop or laptop. Often recoverable if you act early. Choose the problem that sounds most like yours.

🗑️Deleted filesFiles were removed or emptied from the trash. The data may still be on the drive.
1/5 · Safe to DIY
🧹Accidentally formattedYou formatted the drive by mistake. A quick format often leaves files, except on SSDs.
2/5 · Low risk
🔍Not detected / not showing upYour computer does not see the drive at all. The cause may be cable, port or power.
3/5 · Take care
🔊Clicking or grinding noisesA repeated click or grind from a spinning drive. Power it off now to limit damage.
5/5 · Pro territory
🪫Won't power on / deadThe drive shows no lights, no spin or no response when connected.
4/5 · High risk
🔥Water, fire or physical damageThe drive was wet, burned, dropped or crushed. Do not power it on again.
5/5 · Pro territory
🧩Corrupted / RAW / asks to formatWindows or macOS says the drive needs formatting or shows as RAW.
3/5 · Take care
🔐Encrypted or lockedThe drive asks for a password or key, such as BitLocker, FileVault or VeraCrypt.
3/5 · Take care
