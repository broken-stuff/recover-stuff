🔌 USB flash drive · 🧩 Corrupted / RAW / asks to format

Fix a Corrupted USB Flash Drive Without Data Loss

A corrupted USB drive has damaged file information, often after being pulled out too early or from a worn memory. The files are often still there. Making a copy before any repair keeps your options open.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Medium

Formatting or running chkdsk can overwrite the information you need, and a failing drive may get worse with each use.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not click Format when Windows asks you to.
  • Do not run chkdsk or any 'repair' prompt before making an image.
  • Do not install recovery software onto the USB drive.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the imaging keeps failing, the drive disconnects, or it reports the wrong size, stop and consult a professional.

Questions people ask

Why does my USB say I need to format the disk?

The file system information is damaged. Your files may still be present, so do not format until you have a copy.

Could my flash drive be fake?

Possibly. Counterfeit drives claim more space than they have and corrupt data. Test with f3 or h2testw after you recover your files.
