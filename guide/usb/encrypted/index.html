🔌 USB flash drive · 🔐 Encrypted or locked

Recover Data from an Encrypted USB Drive

Encrypted USB drives scramble data so it cannot be read without the password or recovery key. If you do not have either, recovery is not realistic. Many people can find the key in a Microsoft account or a password manager.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Varies

Wrong resets or an erase on a hardware-encrypted stick can destroy the key and make the data permanently unreadable.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not reset or erase the drive if it has a PIN pad or reset option.
  • Do not format it to get access.
  • Do not use tools that claim to crack or guess encryption.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the drive is both encrypted and physically failing, or uses hardware encryption and the controller has died, stop and ask a professional, and expect that it may not be possible.

Questions people ask

Can I recover files from a locked USB without the password?

Realistically no. Good encryption cannot be bypassed, and anyone who promises otherwise should be treated with caution.

Where is my BitLocker key stored?

Often in your Microsoft account online, in a work or school account, on a printout or in a file you saved earlier.
