🔌 USB flash drive · 🔍 Not detected / not showing up

USB Flash Drive Not Detected or Not Showing

A USB drive may not appear because of a bad port, a missing drive letter, damaged files, a worn connector or a failed controller chip. Many cases are simple to check. Real hardware failure is harder and often needs a specialist.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Varies

Basic checks are safe, but forcing a damaged connector or soldering without experience can break the drive for good.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not click Format if Windows offers it.
  • Do not keep bending or wiggling the plug.
  • Do not use heat, glue or a soldering iron on it.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the drive is not detected on any computer, or the plug is broken, ask a professional, who may use a chip-off or monolithic recovery.

Questions people ask

Why does my USB drive show 0 bytes?

The controller often cannot read the memory chip properly. This is usually a hardware fault and not fixable with software.

Can a broken USB plug be fixed?

Sometimes, by soldering on a new connector, but it takes skill and a slip can destroy the data, so leave it to a repair shop or lab.
