🔌 USB flash drive · 🔥 Water, fire or physical damage

USB Flash Drive Damaged by Water or Heat

USB flash drives are fairly tough, and some survive a wash. Liquid, heat or fire can still corrode the board or short the chip. Plugging it in before it is clean and dry can cause a short that ruins it.

4/5 · High risk
Success odds: Varies

Powering on a wet or contaminated drive can short it, and heating it can damage the memory chip.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not plug it in while it is wet or dirty.
  • Do not dry it with a hair dryer, oven, microwave or rice.
  • Do not scrub the contacts or open the case.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the drive is burned, cracked, corroded or does not show up after drying, stop and contact a professional rather than plugging it in again.

Questions people ask

Can a USB drive survive the washing machine?

Often yes, if it is fully dried before use. Waiting several days is safer than plugging it in right away.

Is heat drying safe for a flash drive?

No. Heat can damage the memory chip and the solder joints.
