🔌 USB flash drive · 🗑️ Deleted files

Recover Deleted Files from a USB Flash Drive

When you delete a file from a USB drive, the data usually stays until new files overwrite it. Most flash drives do not use TRIM, so recovery chances are often good if you act quickly. Every new file you save lowers the odds.

2/5 · Low risk
Success odds: High

Scanning from a copy is safe, but saving anything new to the drive can overwrite the deleted files.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Stop saving or copying anything to the USB drive.
  • Do not install recovery software onto the USB drive.
  • Do not format the drive, even if Windows asks you to.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the drive is unstable, disconnects while copying, or the files are critical and scans find nothing, stop and ask a professional.

Questions people ask

Why can I recover deleted files from a flash drive?

Deleting usually just removes the file's listing. The data stays on the drive until something new is written over it.

Do deleted USB files go to the Recycle Bin?

On Windows, usually not: files deleted from flash drives are removed right away. A Mac may keep them in the Trash until you empty it, so check there.
