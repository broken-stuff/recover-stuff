⚡ SSD (SATA and NVMe) · 🔐 Encrypted or locked

Recover Data from an Encrypted SSD

If your SSD is protected with BitLocker, FileVault, VeraCrypt or built-in hardware encryption, the data is scrambled without the key. Without the password or recovery key, recovery is not realistically possible. The good news is that keys are often stored somewhere you can find.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Varies

Wrong attempts, resets, or erasing the drive's encryption can make the data permanently unreadable.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not reset, secure erase or 'crypto erase' the SSD.
  • Do not reinstall the operating system over the encrypted drive.
  • Do not trust any site or service that promises to crack encryption.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the SSD controller fails on a hardware-encrypted drive, or you are locked out with no key, only a professional can say whether anything is possible, and often the answer is no.

Questions people ask

Can encrypted data be recovered without the key?

In practice, no. Modern encryption with a strong password cannot realistically be guessed or bypassed, so beware of anyone claiming to do so.

Where is my BitLocker recovery key?

Often in your Microsoft account online, a work or school account, a printout, or a file saved to a USB stick.
