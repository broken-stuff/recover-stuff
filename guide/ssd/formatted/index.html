⚡ SSD (SATA and NVMe) · 🧹 Accidentally formatted

Recover Data from a Formatted SSD

Formatting an SSD, especially a full format or a reinstall of the operating system, commonly triggers TRIM, which erases the old data. Quick formats on internal SSDs are usually not recoverable. External or older setups that skip TRIM may still hold data.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Low

Reinstalling Windows or writing new files on the SSD speeds up permanent loss, so careless steps can end any chance of recovery.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not copy new files to the SSD or reinstall the operating system on it.
  • Do not install recovery software on the formatted SSD.
  • Do not format it again, even if a prompt suggests it.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the data is irreplaceable and TestDisk and PhotoRec find nothing, a professional is your only remaining option, though success on a trimmed SSD is unlikely.

Questions people ask

Will a quick format on an SSD wipe my files?

Often yes. The format itself keeps data, but TRIM usually follows and erases it. Odds are better on external SSDs or older systems without TRIM.

Should I let Windows format a drive that shows as RAW?

No. Formatting overwrites the structure you need. Image the drive first, then repair or recover from the copy.
