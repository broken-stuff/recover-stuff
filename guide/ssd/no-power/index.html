⚡ SSD (SATA and NVMe) · 🪫 Won't power on / dead

SSD Dead or Won't Power On

A dead SSD may be fine and the problem lies in the cable, port, adapter or power supply. If those check out, the controller or a power component on the SSD may have failed. SSDs have no moving parts, so there is nothing to listen for.

4/5 · High risk
Success odds: Low

Checking cables is safe, but repeatedly applying power to a drive with a short circuit can damage it more.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not keep plugging it in if you smell burning or see scorch marks.
  • Do not try to repair the circuit board yourself.
  • Do not keep power cycling it again and again hoping it comes back.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the SSD is still not detected on a known-good cable and computer, the controller has likely failed and only a professional can attempt recovery.

Questions people ask

How can I tell if my SSD is dead or just the cable?

Test it on a different cable, port and computer. If it fails everywhere, the drive itself is the problem.

Does a dead SSD mean my data is gone?

Not necessarily, but because many SSDs encrypt data and tie it to the controller, recovery is harder than with hard drives.
