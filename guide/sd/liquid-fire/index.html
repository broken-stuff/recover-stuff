📸 SD / microSD card · 🔥 Water, fire or physical damage

SD or microSD Card Damaged by Water or Heat

Many SD cards are rated water resistant, and some survive being washed. Liquid, heat or fire can still corrode the contacts or damage the chip. Plugging in a wet or dirty card can short it.

4/5 · High risk
Success odds: Varies

Powering a wet or contaminated card can short it, and heat can permanently damage the memory chip.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not put the card in a camera, phone or reader while it is wet.
  • Do not use a hair dryer, oven, microwave or rice to dry it.
  • Do not scrub the contacts or take the card apart.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the card is burned, corroded, bent or still not detected after drying, stop and contact a professional rather than trying again.

Questions people ask

Can an SD card survive the washing machine?

Often yes, if fully dried before use. Waiting several days is safer than rushing.

Is it safe to dry a card with heat?

No. Heat can damage the memory chip and its connections.
