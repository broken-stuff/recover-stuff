📸 SD / microSD card · 🔍 Not detected / not showing up

SD or microSD Card Not Detected or Not Reading

A card may not show up because of a dirty or bad reader, a missing drive letter, damaged file information, or a failed controller. Many cases are fixed by changing the reader. If the card is truly dead, only a specialist can try chip-level recovery.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Varies

Simple checks are safe, but accepting a format prompt or forcing a damaged card can destroy what is left.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not click Format when the computer or camera asks.
  • Do not bend, force or clean the card contacts with anything abrasive.
  • Do not keep inserting and removing it many times.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If no reader detects the card, or it reports a tiny size such as 0 MB, the controller or memory has likely failed and needs a lab with flash recovery tools, since most microSD cards are sealed monolith chips.

Questions people ask

Why does my microSD card show nothing in the reader?

The reader, adapter or card contacts may be bad, or the card's controller may have failed. Try a different reader first.

Can a dead SD card be recovered?

Sometimes, by labs that read the memory directly, often by soldering to tiny test points on monolith cards. It is costly and not guaranteed.
