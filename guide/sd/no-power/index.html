📸 SD / microSD card · 🪫 Won't power on / dead

SD or microSD Card Dead and Not Responding

A card cannot show a light or sound, so a dead card just does not respond anywhere. Check the reader, adapter and contacts first. If it is still dead, the controller is likely broken and the data can only be read with special equipment.

4/5 · High risk
Success odds: Low

A cracked or shorted card can be damaged further by repeated tries, and DIY opening or soldering destroys it.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not keep trying if the card feels hot or looks cracked.
  • Do not open the card or try to solder it.
  • Do not format it if it briefly appears and asks.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the card is dead in every reader, monolithic or controller failure usually needs chip-off recovery by a professional.

Questions people ask

Is a dead microSD card always lost?

No, but DIY options are limited. If the memory chip is intact, a lab with special tools may read it.

Could it just be my card reader?

Yes, that is common. Try a different reader and adapter before giving up.
