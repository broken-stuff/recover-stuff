📸 SD / microSD card · 🗑️ Deleted files

Recover Deleted Photos from an SD or microSD Card

When you delete files from an SD card, the data usually stays until new photos or videos overwrite it. SD cards do not use TRIM in most cameras and readers, so recovery odds are often good. The most important step is to stop using the card.

2/5 · Low risk
Success odds: High

Scanning a copy is safe, but taking even a few more photos can overwrite what you want back.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Stop taking photos or videos with the card right now.
  • Do not install recovery software onto the card.
  • Do not format the card, even if the camera or phone asks you to.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the card is unstable, disconnects during copying, or the photos are irreplaceable and scans find nothing, stop and consult a professional.

Questions people ask

Can I recover photos I deleted from a camera card?

Often yes, if you stopped using the card. Photos are usually still on it until new ones overwrite them.

Does taking more photos hurt my chances?

Yes. New pictures can be written over the deleted ones, so stop using the card immediately.
