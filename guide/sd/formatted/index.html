📸 SD / microSD card · 🧹 Accidentally formatted

Recover Photos from a Formatted SD or microSD Card

Formatting in most cameras clears the file list but leaves most photos on the card. Formatting in a phone, a full format in Windows, or a camera's 'low-level' or 'secure' format often erases the data and lowers the odds.

2/5 · Low risk
Success odds: Varies

Recovery scans are safe on a copy, but taking new photos or formatting again overwrites the old data.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not take any more photos or videos with the card.
  • Do not format it again or choose a 'secure' or 'full' format.
  • Do not install recovery software onto the card.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the card was fully formatted, reused heavily after formatting, or the images fail to copy, a professional may help, but chances are lower.

Questions people ask

Do formatted SD cards keep my photos?

Usually a quick format does. Most photos remain until new ones are written, so recovery is often possible.

Should I format the card again to fix it?

Not until your files are safely recovered. Another format can overwrite more data.
