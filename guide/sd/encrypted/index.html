📸 SD / microSD card · 🔐 Encrypted or locked

Recover Data from an Encrypted SD or microSD Card

Some phones, cameras and apps encrypt memory cards, tying them to a device, a password or a key. If you do not have the key, recovery is not realistically possible. If you do, the data can usually be unlocked on the right device.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Varies

Resetting the phone, formatting the card or removing the card's encryption can permanently destroy the key link and the data.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not format the card or reset the phone or camera it was used in.
  • Do not use tools that claim to crack or guess passwords.
  • Do not install recovery software onto the card.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the card is both encrypted and damaged, or you lost the key, a professional can only say whether anything is possible, and often it is not.

Questions people ask

Can I read an encrypted SD card without the key?

Realistically no. Modern encryption cannot be guessed or bypassed, so be wary of anyone who says otherwise.

Will a new phone read my encrypted card?

Often not, if the key was tied to the old phone. Keep the old device until you have copied your files.
