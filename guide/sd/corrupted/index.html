📸 SD / microSD card · 🧩 Corrupted / RAW / asks to format

Fix a Corrupted SD or microSD Card Without Losing Photos

A card can become corrupted when removed during a write, when power dies, or when it wears out. The photos are often still there even if the camera shows an error. Making a copy before any repair keeps your options open.

3/5 · Take care
Success odds: Medium

Formatting, chkdsk or the camera's 'repair' prompts can overwrite the information needed to get your files back.

✋ Stop! Don't do this first

  • Do not click Format when the camera or computer asks to.
  • Do not run chkdsk or any 'repair' prompt before making an image.
  • Do not take more photos with the card.

🧑‍🔧 When to call a pro

If the card disconnects often, reports the wrong size, or imaging fails, stop and ask a professional.

Questions people ask

Why does my camera say the card needs formatting?

The file system is damaged or unreadable. Your photos may still be on the card, so do not format yet.

Could my SD card be counterfeit?

It might be, especially if the price was too good. Fake cards claim more space than they have, so test with f3 or h2testw after recovery.
