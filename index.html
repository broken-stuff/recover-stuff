Lost your files? Let's get them back.

Recover Stuff is a free, step-by-step guide that helps everyday people try to get files back from hard drives, SSDs, USB sticks, SD cards and external drives. Pick your device, then pick what happened, and you get a plain-language plan with free tools and safety warnings.

Most important rule: stop using the device right now.
